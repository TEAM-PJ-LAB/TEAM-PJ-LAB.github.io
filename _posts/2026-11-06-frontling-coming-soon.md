Frontling is on the way.

[Frontling](/frontling/) is Team PJ's next service: help for US college admissions, built on verified research. It starts from a published research paper and turns it into a project a high school student can actually finish, sized to their grade, weeks and laptop. Coaching comes as hints, never as the answer, so the research in a student's application is their own.

## Where it stands

Frontling is in development and not available yet. We'll post here on the board as soon as it opens, so please wait a little longer.

## Want to try it first?

For our first pilot, we're looking for high school students preparing to apply to US colleges, and their parents. Write to [pj.southkorea@gmail.com](mailto:pj.southkorea@gmail.com?subject=%5BFrontling%5D%20Pilot), or read more on the [Frontling page](/frontling/).
