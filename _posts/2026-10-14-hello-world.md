Today we're opening PJ Group's website.

PJ Group is a software company from South Korea, started by two student founders — Jung Woojin (DGIST) and Park Bugeon (high school). We build products, and grow the ones that work into businesses of their own.

## What we're building

- **ANABADA** — evidence-backed fair prices for second-hand goods, from a few photos.
- **Ryeong-Eon** *(working title)* — a verified AI memorial voice service.
- **SampleLedger** — checkout, return and traceability for physical samples.

More on each one in [Apps](/apps/).

## Our one rule

**Every app and web service we build launches within a month.**

We'll post launch notes and build logs here. If you're an investor or want to build with us, write to [pj.southkorea@gmail.com](mailto:pj.southkorea@gmail.com) — or leave a comment below.
